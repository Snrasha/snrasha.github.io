Formation Save Mod
Download (.dll)

[Overview]
  • This mod allows you to save and load your formations.


[Specifications]
  • Linked to Save Slots: Formation data is saved and tied to the slot number of your in-game save data.
  • Load Condition: When loading a formation, if the first ship cannot be placed, the formation load process will not be executed.


[Notes / Warnings]
  • Hangar Slot Mismatch: A warning will be displayed if a unit's hangar number (placement) during loading differs from when the formation was saved.
  • Caution on Selling Identical Units: If you have multiple units of the same model deployed and sell one of them, please be aware that the pilots assigned to those models may end up swapping the units they are piloting.
  • Other: There may be other unexpected behaviors (such as unknown bugs) besides the ones listed above.
